Your nationality application, up to date.

An extension for Chrome and Firefox that checks the status of your Spanish nationality-by-residence application: it fills in the official form for you, leaves you only the captcha, and tells you as soon as the status changes.

  • Free
  • No account
  • Your data stays in your browser
  • Independent, not official

Reminder, captcha, result

Checking your status stops being a chore: you save your details once, and every check comes down to three steps.

  1. It reminds you

    Save one application or several and pick a reminder: the days and the time you want to check. When it is due, a browser notification tells you.

  2. You solve the captcha

    NacioCheck opens the official status page and fills in the form with your details. Only the captcha is left on screen: you solve it, as the site asks, and the check is sent right then.

    NacioCheck never solves, skips or sends the captcha anywhere. A person always solves it: you.

  3. You see the result

    The status the official page shows goes into your history. If it has changed since last time, NacioCheck tells you loud and clear and explains what it usually means.

The usual path of an application

A guide, not official. These explanations sum up what has been seen on real files and in public sources. They are not legal advice and do not replace the official notification, which arrives through DEHú.

  1. Abierto - Registrado Open, registered: the application is in and registered. Processing starts here.
  2. Abierto - En tramitación Su expediente se encuentra en tramitación In progress, before the mandatory reports are requested.
  3. Abierto - En tramitación Pendiente solicitud informes preceptivos oficiales The mandatory reports have been requested and the file is waiting for them.
  4. Abierto - En tramitación Pendiente recepción de informes preceptivos oficiales The reports are pending arrival: the step after requesting them.
  5. Abierto - En tramitación Su expediente se encuentra en calificación On the files observed, the decision usually comes within days or weeks from here.
  6. Concedido - Enviada notificación Granted: the decision is favourable and the notification has been sent through DEHú.
  7. Finalizado - Concedido, notificado Grant notified. What remains (registration, oath or promise) happens at the Registro Civil.

Your data is yours

Your NIE, your file number and your date of birth have no reason to leave your computer or phone. NacioCheck is built so that they do not.

  • Nothing leaves your browser

    Your details and your history are stored only in the extension. There is no account and no server of ours: the only request is the check you send to the official site.

  • Encryption, if you want it

    You can encrypt your details with a passphrase only you know. You can also export them to a file or delete everything whenever you like.

  • No tracking

    The extension has no analytics, no ads and no telemetry. We do not know who uses it or what they check.

  • Open source

    NacioCheck is free software under the GPL-3.0: anyone can read what it does and how.

Frequently asked questions

Is it an official tool?

No. NacioCheck is a personal, independent project with no link to the Ministerio de Justicia or any public body. It uses the official status page as it is, and the status explanations are only a guide.

Does it solve the captcha for me?

No. You solve the captcha, every time. NacioCheck fills in the form and waits; it does not skip the captcha, solve it or send it to any service.

Where is my data stored?

Only in your browser, in the extension's local storage. You can encrypt it with a passphrase, export it to a file or delete it from Options. Nothing is sent to us.

Is it free?

Yes. Every current feature is free and will stay free. No ads and no account.

Which browsers does it work in?

Chrome and Firefox on the desktop. Firefox for Android is a target that has not been tested on a device yet. The install links will appear above as soon as the stores publish it.

Are you an immigration agency or a law firm?

NacioCheck is free for everyone. If you need more, such as following many applications or tools of your own for your team, let's talk.

Always with the same rule: a person solves the captcha.
