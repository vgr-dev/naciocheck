NacioCheck privacy policy

Applies to the NacioCheck extension 0.1.0 and later. Last reviewed: 16 November 2026.

Summary

NacioCheck keeps your applications and the history of checks in your browser only. There are no accounts, no servers of our own, no analytics and no advertising. The extension does not send your data anywhere.

What is stored, and where

Everything is stored with the browser's storage API, inside the extension's profile:

  • Local storage (storage.local, key naciocheck): the applications you add (the name you recognise them by, and the NIE, the application number, the year and the date of birth — or those same details encrypted), the history of checks (date, outcome, the status text the site returned, and whether you have seen it) and the settings (reminder and language).
  • Session storage (storage.session): the encryption passphrase, while the browser is open. The browser clears that storage when it closes and the passphrase is never written to disk.

The history lives in the browser profile: signing out of the browser or clearing its data can lose it. That is what the backup is for.

What it does not do

  • It does not create accounts and it has no servers of its own.
  • It does not send your data anywhere: no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising, no tracking.
  • It does not use the browser's synchronised storage, so your data does not travel to Google or Mozilla.
  • It does not solve the captcha or send it to any service: you tick it. The official site loads Google's reCAPTCHA on its own page, and that happens on that page, not in the extension.
  • When you start a check, your browser submits the form to the electronic site (sede.mjusticia.gob.es) exactly as if you had filled it in by hand. That is the query you asked for, and it goes to the official site, not to us.

Optional encryption

You can protect the NIE, the application number, the year and the date of birth with a passphrase of your own, from Options. They are encrypted with AES-GCM and a key derived from your passphrase with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA-256 (600,000 iterations). The passphrase is never stored on disk: without it, those details cannot be read.

If you forget the passphrase, they cannot be recovered. The history of checks stays, but you will have to type the applications again. Nobody, not even the person who maintains the project, can decrypt them.

Backup, export and delete

  • Export (Options → Backup): downloads a JSON file with what you have stored. If the data is not encrypted, the extension warns you first. You keep that file wherever you like, and it is the only way your data can leave the browser.
  • Import: reads a backup file and merges it with what you already have.
  • Delete one application (Options → Delete): removes the application and its checks.
  • Delete everything: uninstall the extension, or clear its data from the browser (chrome://extensions → NacioCheck → Remove; about:addons → NacioCheck → Remove in Firefox). Uninstalling makes the browser delete the extension's storage.

Children and third parties

NacioCheck is not aimed at children and collects nobody's data: there is nothing to pass on and nobody to share it with. No data is sold or transferred to third parties, because none is collected.

Changes to this policy

If what the extension stores or sends changes, this page changes before that version is published, and the review date above will say so. The project's history is in the repository.

Contact

For privacy questions, write on the extension's listing in the Chrome Web Store or addons.mozilla.org, or open an issue in the project's repository (github.com/vgr-dev/naciocheck).

Not affiliated

NacioCheck is a personal, independent project. It is not affiliated with the Ministerio de Justicia, the Government of Spain or any public body, and it does not represent any of them. NacioCheck is not an official tool and its information does not replace the official resolution of your application.
